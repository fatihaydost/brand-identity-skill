# Trademarks

This repository mentions real companies, products and organisations in a few places, most of all in the colour
library under `skills/brand-identity/assets/library/` (colours read from public websites, with the brand's name and
site). Every such name, logo, trade dress and colour belongs to its owner. Nothing here is affiliated with, sponsored
by or endorsed by any of them.

## What the data is

- Colours are **approximations of what a site's CSS rendered on the day it was measured**. They are not the owners'
  brand specifications, and they drift whenever the site changes.
- The library is **reference material**: it shows which hues and lightness patterns a sector already uses, so a new
  identity can find open space. It is named and used for that purpose only.
- Font names (for example Google Fonts families) are used to identify the typefaces. Each family keeps its own
  licence, which the skill checks and states in its output.

## What the licence does not give you

- The MIT licence covers this repository's code and original writing. It transfers **no rights** in any third-party
  name, logo, colour or trade dress.
- The skill is built to steer away from imitation: it is told to look for what a category leaves unclaimed and never
  to copy a competitor's identity. Do not use another company's colours, marks or type treatment to suggest a link
  with that company.
- The skill cannot clear a mark or a colour for registration. Protection depends on the jurisdiction and on how a
  mark is used. If a trademark search matters for your brand, ask a trademark professional.
- Pantone and other proprietary colour systems are named only where the user supplies a reference. None of their
  data is distributed here.

## Fictional examples

The briefs in `evals/fixtures/` and the example brands shown in this repository use invented businesses.
Any likeness to a real business is accidental.

## Removal requests

If you own one of the marks and want an entry taken out, open an issue and it will be removed promptly.
