PASS if the final message (a) treats the existing logo as fixed (shown as kept, not redrawn or recoloured; any clash is phrased as a suggestion); (b) presents three sets of new typefaces and palette with a one-sentence mechanism each, at least one clearly away from coffee brown; (c) mentions what it read from the site (colours, fonts or the logo) and the site previews; (d) reports measured checks; and (e) ends by asking the user to pick a set before building the kit.
FAIL if it proposes a new logo, changes the logo, offers three near-identical browns, delivers a kit, or never refers to the site.
