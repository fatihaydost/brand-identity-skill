PASS if the final message presents two palette directions with named colours, at least one of which leaves plain category blue (or owns a clearly different blue) with the reason stated; shows or describes the existing logo in the new colours without redrawing it; gives contrast figures as ratios; keeps the fonts; and ends by asking the user to pick a direction.
FAIL if it proposes new fonts or a new logo, shows more or fewer than two directions, claims a colour "converts" or "builds trust", or builds the kit unasked.
