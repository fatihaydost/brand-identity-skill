# Cohesion

One voice per set, real difference between sets. Tags: `[strong]` `[limited]` `[practice]`.

## Mechanism and expression move
- **Mechanism:** one sentence naming the single visual idea that yields the mark, the type choice and the palette
  together ("a ledger's ruled line: hairline-cut wordmark, rule-thin symbol, ink-on-paper palette"): one idea,
  three expressions. `[practice]`
- **Expression move:** exactly one loud component. Expressive mark → quiet type and palette; expressive palette →
  plain mark; expressive type → minimal graphics. `[practice]`
- Cohesion is consistency with the strategy, not "everything in one style"; art directors also seek an *ownable*
  element against competitors ([Phillips et al. 2014](https://doi.org/10.1080/00913367.2013.867824)). `[limited]`
  Mark taste-driven choices as taste in `rationale`.

## Axes (`identitylib.AXES`, −2 first pole … +2 second pole)
Pick 2–4 per brief from the user's adjectives or site signals; the user may correct them.

| Axis | Logo | Type | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| `warm_cool` | round terminals, organic curves ↔ sharp, geometric | humanist ↔ grotesk, geometric | hue family + neutral tint |
| `classic_contemporary` | monogram, serif ↔ abstract, wordmark | old-style serif ↔ neo-grotesk, variable | category code kept ↔ broken |
| `playful_serious` | moving forms ↔ static, symmetric | elaborate, high x-height ↔ regular | high chroma, many hues ↔ low chroma, 1–2 |
| `premium_accessible` | few parts, fine lines, space ↔ bold, filled | light weights, contrast serif ↔ bold sans | dark, few colours ↔ bright, several |
| `quiet_loud` | small mark ↔ big, dominant | one family, narrow scale ↔ display + text, wide scale | neutral-led ↔ coloured surfaces |
| `friend_authority` | hand-made ↔ institutional | rounded sans ↔ serif/grotesk | warm light ground ↔ dark saturated ground |

Axes adapt GV's brand-sprint sliders ([Reforge](https://www.reforge.com/blog/brief-how-to-define-your-brand-with-gv-s-3-hour-sprint));
"real personality is at the ends" ([Learning Loop](https://learningloop.io/plays/workshop-exercise/personality-sliders)).
The "carries" columns are `[practice]`. The shared vocabulary: logo design dimensions (natural, harmony, elaborate;
[Henderson & Cote 1998](https://doi.org/10.1177/002224299806200202)) recur in type
([Henderson et al. 2004](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkg.68.4.60.42736); weight, flourish shift brand personality,
[Grohmann et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1057/bm.2012.23)). `[limited]` Scores are reasoned judgement, not
measurement.

## Making sets differ (warnings, heuristic, uncalibrated)
- Sets sit at different ends of **≥ 2 axes**; three sets in the middle are one idea three times.
- Each component carries the difference: palette (hue family or light vs dark structure), type (class or clear
  width/weight), logo (mark type: wordmark, letterform, symbol). Differ in **strategy**, not only hue.
- Inside a set, component positions stay within 1 step; a bigger gap is deliberate contrast with a written reason.
- Refresh: one axis becomes distance from the current identity, evolution → revolution.
- Three routes, one RECOMMENDED: too many options hurt choice when sets are complex and preferences unclear
  ([Chernev et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcps.2014.08.002)). `[strong]`

## Colour × type
- Font colour and font design effects on brand personality are **independent** (Grohmann 2013, study 3)
  `[limited]`; that they add up when aimed at one trait is our inference.
- Thin or unusual faces render fainter than their colour
  ([W3C Understanding 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), informative). Our
  heuristic: weight ≤ 300 or a hairline serif gets 4.5:1 even when large.
- Light text on dark looks bolder; dark mode often drops weight or uses negative GRAD
  ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/adapting-typography-to-user-preferences-with-css)). `[practice]` Reading
  speed does not require it ([Palmén et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1145/3544548.3581552)). `[limited]`
- Saturated accents vibrate in small text; keep them for display sizes and fills. `[practice]`

## Mixing (`brand.py mix`)
Clients pick parts from several routes and get a "Frankencomp" ([Warren, ALA 2012](https://alistapart.com/article/style-tiles-and-how-they-work/)).
`[practice]` Mix on request only: build set D, re-colour D's logo to D's palette, re-score axes and show the
cohesion warning. A conflict with a kept component is a suggestion; the kept part never changes.
